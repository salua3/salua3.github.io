Amphetamines


Amphetamines, by Salua. Introducing the ebook Amphetamines, based on a presentation I prepared during my last year of Pharmacy, at the University of Granada, Spain, published in 2008. For this project, I decided to focus on the effects of amphetamines on the brain. The reason for this is that I lived in Granada, and there is a lot of drug abuse like methamphetamines to reduce exam stress, and also in college I am increasingly surprised by the amount of people prescribed to, and using variations of the same stimulant like Adderall or Ritalin, also a lot of people use it for off label purposes or even without a prescription. Amphetamines are central nervous system stimulants first synthesized in 1887. They act by increasing the neurotransmitters dopamine, serotonin, and norepinephrine. They can produce both helpful effects like happiness and energy, as well as harmful effects like addiction, aggression, and overdose risks. While they have various therapeutic uses such as treating narcolepsy and Adhd, amphetamines also carry misuse risks if used illegally or without medical supervision. The project covers the following topics. In spanish. History of amphetamines. Definition. Types of amphetamines. How amphetamines work. High dose users. Psychological effects.
