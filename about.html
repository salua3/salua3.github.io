About Me

Hi, my name is Salua, from Tetouan, Morocco. I want to share with you more about my journey, discovering my passion for art, science and writing, sharing insights, inspiration and maybe a few amusing anecdotes along the way. I’ve had this desire to build a portfolio website to showcase all my various creative work for many years now. Join me as I explore the amazing intersection of art, science and storytelling, reconnecting with my creative passions, rebuilding my creative life, that creative spark and the milestones I achieved along the way. Whether you’re an artist, a designer, a writer, a business owner, or just someone looking to get back in touch with a passion you’ve lost, I hope my page will inspire you to jump-start your own creative journey. To purchase or ask me anything please contact me. Sally.
