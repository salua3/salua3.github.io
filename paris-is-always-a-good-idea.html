Paris is always a good idea

by Salua April 25,2025

Paris is always a good idea. Paris is always a good idea as it's one of the rare cities in the world that gives you that undeniable feeling of inner happiness and love. My first visit to Paris was a remarkable experience, which I will never forget. I booked the flight with my sister Jiji from Granada in the cold Christmas weather. We intended to stay until the New Year in a hotel located in a suburb of Paris and visit the city every day. It was a great feeling to arrive in Paris and see the city of light, a modern city with a very deep sense of history. I will never forget when I first saw the monuments. It was like a huge open-air museum. It was love at first sight. Paris was everything I dreamed of and more. The city was beautiful, busy, full of people, wine, cheese, and bakeries around every corner. The first time I saw the Eiffel Tower it took my breath away. And I could see it right out the front doors of our hotel. That first night as I stood on the street and watched those twinkling lights I shed a few tears. My Paris experience. Ever since I can remember, I’ve always dreamed of going to Paris. I have always admired French art, whether it was in the form of a sculpture or a film. One of my favorite things about Paris was not just seeing the Eiffel Tower or the other amazing historical sights, but it was connecting with myself on a deeper level. The fact of being in Paris with Jiji, and being in the same place where my parents spent their honeymoon, and where ilsa and rick met, it really touched my heart. What does we’ll always have Paris mean? The line comes from the classic movie, Casablanca, 1942, that stars Humphrey Bogart and Ingrid Bergman, ilsa and rick, along with several other brilliant actors. You really need to watch the whole film, and visit Rick's Café. We’ll always have Paris, it’s a movie line. Casablanca, as others have said. Sometimes people say it when something profound has happened between them, even if it wasn’t specifically Paris. But it always refers to an experience with someone else that you treasure in your heart for the rest of your life. My sister and I went to Paris for a few days. The memory of being in Paris, the most beautiful city in the world, with each other, is something we’ll always have, the rest of our lives, no matter what happens in the future. As long as we have memories and life, we’ll have Paris. If you can make it to Paris, you should do it. It’s easier to understand this line, we'll always have Paris, if you’ve been there with someone you love. Bogart and Bergman have had a love affair in Paris some years earlier in the plot. However, Bergman is married to a fictional anti-German French resistance leader named Victor Laszlo, played by Paul Henreid. In reality, Henreid was playing a natural role since he actually was a brave opponent of the Germans. He was strongly anti-Nazi, so much so that he was designated an official enemy of the Third Reich. Anyway, getting back to the plot of Casablanca, there is now this competing love triangle where Bergman is torn between her love and sense of duty to her husband, Laszlo, and her passion for Bogy, kindled by their affair in Paris. After much drama, in the closing scene, Bogy ultimately plays the hero by sending his once-in-a-lifetime love, Bergman, off with her husband Laszlo so that he can most effectively continue the fight against the Germans. Bogy does this because, when the two main characters meet in Paris, they fall in love. The line means that regardless of everything else that happens, they shall always have those moments memories from Paris and nothing can change that. It’s the right thing to do, he sacrifices his, and her, personal feelings for the greater cause of defeating the Germans. We can now say the classic line, We’ll always have Paris. Salua.
