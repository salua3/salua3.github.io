Sally's Arty Stuff


Sally's arty stuff. Hi, my name is Salua. Welcome to my art gallery, I figured it was time for me to be brave and start sharing my art more publicly. I hope you will love and enjoy this journey. View gallery. My love for the beauty of nature and color is what truly inspires me. What is more important, the process or the result? I used to believe for a long time it was the result, but now I don't care. I'm painting for fun, and whatever happens, happens. Painting isn’t just about starting a business, I love the vibe it gives off, it's also a great way to de-stress and create unique gifts for friends and family. The first series of artworks I created is dedicated to the theme of nature: illustrations of atmospheric landscapes, animals, plants, and the change of seasons, some in watercolor and others in oil, using natural pigments. Traces of the unspoken is my latest acrylic glass painting, a super colorful palette with floral, abstract, and landscape designs, on the challenging material of glass. Sometimes I like doing these more minimalist paintings, as I think they add a certain level of dreaminess. These works of art are perfect for those who appreciate clean lines, vibrant colors, simplicity, and fun seasonal decor using natural materials. Originals and prints are available. Learn more about how I make my own color palette. To purchase, please contact me.
