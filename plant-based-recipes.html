Ebook by Salua

Plant based recipes


Hi, my name is Salua.
Welcome to my 'Plant Based Recipes' ebook.

This is a plant-based digital nutrition guide that I released in spring 2019 after finishing my pharmacy & nutrition studies in Granada, and it's designed to help people to a healthy eating lifestyle, in hopes to inspire everyone to simply eat more plants.

This ebook includes my all-time favourite recipes that are simple, healthy and delicious, to nourish your body, including breakfasts, mains, desserts, snacks, smoothies and juices. If you are starting your self-love journey, I'd highly recommend checking it out.

Going vegan isn't always easy, sometimes It’s quite difficult to know where to start! Plant-based eating can feel a lot simpler in the summer as there’s a plentiful supply of fresh vegetables, fruits and salads. But as the colder nights set in we all feel the need to load up on warmer and more hearty dishes.

Christmas can also be a difficult time. Finding good plant-based alternatives to our festive favourites is something I’ve struggled with in the past. That’s why I wanted to create and share vegan options for some of my favourite winter meals. No one should have to settle for second best when getting cosy or celebrating with family. Cooking plant-based classics for yourself or loved ones doesn’t mean just eating a plate of boring veg!

I’ve made sure that all the ingredients I’ve used are easy to find at the supermarket, the recipes are simple to follow and the finished dishes will even impress your non-veggie friends! I hope you enjoy them as much as I do.

What can I expect?
35 individual vegan recipes 7 chapters including soups, heart-warming meals, roast dinner mains, sides, sauces, desserts and hot drinks, basic ingredients that are easy to find in a supermarket, step-by-step instructions, photographs of every dish.
