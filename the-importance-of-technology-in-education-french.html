Hi, my name is Salua. Welcome to 'The importance of technology in education' ebook (French version). This is my final project of computer science studies. I did it in french and it is entitled 'L'importance de la technologie dans l'éducation' which means 'The importance of technology in education'.

The project covers the following topics:

How is technology reconnecting with education?
Do technologies have a real impact on academic success?
Use of technology in education to learn and teach.
New technologies at the service of education. Malala Yousafzai.
How important are computers in education?
