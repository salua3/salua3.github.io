Mamma mia

by Salua December 30,2024

Mamma mia. It’s my mom’s birthday today, so I want to dedicate this post to her stating the reasons why I enjoy and love being with her. Mom has been around for a lot, in my first steps, school and career, relationship rollercoasters. She has given me invaluable guidance and support, and she’s more than deserving of a well-considered token of appreciation on her birthday. Of course, sure-fire bets like flowers, chocolates, and stylish basics will lift her spirits, or a heartfelt thank-you note can go a long way in letting her know how much I appreciate her, but on this occasion, to celebrate, I wanted to do something a little different, a unique handmade gift from me, I thought it would be fun to put together some of my favorite craft and beauty projects that say I love you and she absolutely loved them. Mom always cherishes anything made with love, it’s never a bad time to tell her how much I love her. Last year we traveled together and had one of the best experiences. I have lots of incredible photos and memories, she took hundreds of photos of me until I got the perfect one, and she didn’t complain about it. When I was young, I never thought about traveling with my mom, it felt awkward and restrictive. But as I grow older, I become more sentimental, understanding, and appreciative. It may be because she is getting older or I matured or I am just making up for the lost time. Salua.

It’s my mom’s birthday today, so I want to dedicate this post to her stating the reasons why I enjoy and love being with her.

Mom has been around for a lot, in my first steps, school and career, relationship rollercoasters... She has given me invaluable guidance and support, and she’s more than deserving of a well-considered token of appreciation on her birthday.

Mom always cherishes anything made with love, it’s never a bad time to tell her how much I love her.

Last year we traveled together and had one of the best experiences. I have lots of incredible photos and memories, she took hundreds of photos of me until I got the perfect one, and she didn’t complain about it.

When I was young, I never thought about traveling with my mom, it felt awkward and restrictive. But as I grow older, I become more sentimental, understanding, and appreciative. It may be because she is getting older or I matured or I am just making up for the lost time…
